# Lume artwork

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool, with transparent backgrounds. Six 1254 × 1254 PNG sprites are stored in `Sources/SeeUsage/Resources/Companion/`. Each expression references the original idle image to preserve the character's identity.

## Idle

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original transparent 2D desktop-pet game sprite for SeeUsage, a macOS app that watches usage quotas.
Primary request: design Lume, an original tiny living origami firefly. Design a distinctive mascot from scratch; use no reference image and do not imitate existing software mascots or famous characters.
Character design: a compact vertically oriented dark aubergine bean / seed body that is also the face, NOT a robot helmet. Two small warm-cream expressive eyes sit directly on the upper body, one slightly narrower than the other, with a tiny off-center cheeky smile. A bright warm amber circular lantern belly glows on the lower body: its light is the creature's stored energy. Two radically different folded-paper wings create the signature silhouette: a broad pale-mint crescent-shaped wing on the left, and a smaller sharp coral kite-shaped wing on the right. The wings extend upward and outward like an asymmetric little origami moth. One thin curled mint antenna ends in a warm gold dot above the head; two tiny gold peg feet hang underneath. Personality: curious, slightly mischievous, friendly, an indie-game spirit, not a generic cute robot. Neutral idle floating pose, front-facing with only a slight three-quarter turn to the right.
Style: confident crisp flat 2D illustration, strong simple geometric silhouette, clean chunky dark aubergine contour, just one or two flat cel-shaded planes per paper wing, no photorealism, no glossy 3D finish, no complex textures. Clear at 64–72 pixels tall. Beautiful restrained warm/cool contrast: aubergine, pale mint, coral, warm amber, cream.
Composition: exactly ONE whole character centered on a square transparent canvas. Character occupies about 68% of canvas height, including antenna and feet. Leave generous consistent transparent margins on all sides for animation. Complete wings, antenna, and feet fully inside canvas. No ground or cast shadow plane.
Constraints: actual transparent alpha background; no lettering, no numbers, no interface, no watermark. No cloud silhouette, no white puffy body, no visor, no astronaut suit, no gloves, no chest diamond, no brand logo. The paper firefly itself is the only subject.
